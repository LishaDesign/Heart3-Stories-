How this app works

Choose a story, then follow the glowing line from your first idea to a finished book.

  • The star: Every story is a glowing firefly around the little star in the middle. Tap one to open it. + New story adds one next to the star, and + Branch from this story adds one that grows out from the story you are in.
  • The six steps: Under the star, follow the glowing line: 1 Ideas, 2 Write and refine, 3 Pages and illustrations, 4 Review, 5 Publish or print, 6 Keep it safe. Tap a step to open or close it. A step turns gold with a tick when it has something in it.
  • Notes: The Notes button in the corner of every step opens a notepad just for that step. A small gold dot shows when it has something written in it.
  • Ideas and dictating: Choose your language near the top, then tap Start dictating in any step and speak. Save each idea, and add them to your draft when you are ready. While dictating you can say full stop, comma, question mark, exclamation mark or new paragraph, and the screen stays awake.
  • Write and refine: Dictate or type the whole story in the draft. Read aloud lets you hear it. Copy it into a chat with Claude to polish it, then paste the improved version back.
  • Pages and illustrations: Split draft into pages makes the pages for you. Then add an illustration to each page. You can put the writing over the picture, drag the box where you want it, and drag its round corner to change the size.
  • Review and publish: Choose the format and page colour, check the list, and tap Preview book to flip through the pages. Then tap Export to publish in step 5 to get a PDF for each format.

About each step

  • 1. Ideas: Dictate your story as it comes. The first thing you say is the start, and each part you save follows on in order. Your saved parts build up under Your story so far.
  • 2. Write and refine: Turn your ideas into the story. Dictate or type the whole story here, then polish it. Use Tidy up to fix capital letters, spacing, repeated words, “um” and “uh” and missing full stops (Undo brings your old version back). Edit by paragraph lets you fix, move, join or delete one paragraph at a time. For a deeper polish, tap Copy for Claude, paste it into a chat with Claude, then paste the improved story back into the draft.
  • 3. Pages and illustrations: Split your draft into pages, then add the writing and an illustration for each page.
  • 4. Review: Choose how the book will look, check the list, then preview it page by page.
  • 5. Publish or print: Make PDF files that are ready to publish or print. The format, page colour and title page are chosen in step 4. Print sizes: Square 8 x 8 in, Horizontal 9 x 6 in, Vertical 6 x 9 in. Pages are made at 200 dpi.
  • 6. Keep it safe: Save a story as a file to move it into a newer version of this app, or to keep a backup. Pages, illustrations and notes are included.
  • Language: Switch the language between recordings if you mix Afrikaans and English.

Moving stories to a newer version of this app

Your stories are saved inside this browser only, so a new version of the app won't see them until you move them across.

  1. Open the story you want to keep and open step 6. Keep it safe.
  2. Tap Export this story, or Export all stories to take everything, including the branches.
  3. On an iPhone, choose Save to Files in the share sheet. Otherwise the file goes to your downloads. Keep the file somewhere safe. It ends in .json.
  4. Open the new version of the app and open step 6. Keep it safe.
  5. Tap Import stories and choose the file. The story appears on the tree with all its pages, illustrations and notes.

Tips: Export before you replace the app or clear your browser data. Importing the same file twice makes a second copy. If the app says storage is full, remove a few illustrations or export and delete older stories.
